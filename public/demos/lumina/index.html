We believe architecture is the physical manifestation of cultural values. Our studio focuses on reducing noise to amplify experience.
Lumina operates at the intersection of brutalist honesty and warm minimalism. We design spaces that respond to their environment, utilizing raw materials that age beautifully alongside the people who inhabit them.
